The Analysis

8 layers of evidence. Then it argues against itself.

Before Thesis reaches a verdict on a company, it weighs every one of these angles, then stress-tests its own conclusion before it makes the call.

01

Fundamental Analysis

Revenue growth, margins, balance sheet strength, and earnings quality — read in the context of the company's sector.

02

Valuation Models

Fair value from a discounted cash flow model across bear, base and bull scenarios, cross-checked against the company's own valuation history. Upside or downside to fair value clearly stated.

03

Earnings Transcript Analysis

Management commentary, guidance changes, and tone shifts analysed from the last 4 earnings calls.

04

Congressional Trades

Recent stock trades by members of Congress, with context on timing and size.

05

Super Investor Holdings

What top fund managers are doing with this stock — adding, trimming, or exiting — and what that signals.

06

Macro & Sector Context

Interest rate sensitivity, sector tailwinds and headwinds, and how the macro environment affects this specific business.

07

Sentiment & Market Signals

News flow, analyst consensus and price targets, and price action — what the market expects versus what the evidence shows.

08

Risks & Bear Case

The case against the stock gets its full say. The biggest risks are rated by stakes and likelihood, with what would make each better or worse.

FAQ

Questions about AI stock research.

How Thesis helps you research a company before you invest.

What is Thesis?

Thesis is an AI stock research app for iOS and Android. You search a US-listed stock and Thesis researches it for you across 8 layers of evidence, including fundamentals, valuation, earnings calls, market research and a bear case, then gives you a clear verdict with a conviction level and fair value.

How do I research a stock before investing with Thesis?

Type the ticker symbol of a US-listed stock, start the analysis, and read the finished report. Each report covers the company's fundamentals, a breakdown of its valuation, digests of its recent earnings calls, an overview of the business, the thesis behind the verdict, and the key risks to watch.

What does an AI stock analysis report include?

Each report appears right on the stock's page: a verdict (strong buy, buy, hold, sell or strong sell) with a conviction level of high, medium or low, fair value and time horizon, a valuation breakdown with bull, base and bear scenarios and their assumptions, the thesis details and research pillars behind the call, the key risks to watch rated by stakes and likelihood, digests of the last four earnings calls, a company overview, and ten years of fundamentals.

Which stocks can I analyze?

Thesis covers 1,500+ US-listed stocks, including US listings of international companies such as ASML, Novo Nordisk and Spotify. Search by ticker symbol or company name.

How long does a stock research report take?

A full report takes about 7 minutes. Track its progress from your home screen, or leave the app and get a push notification when the report is ready.

How much does Thesis cost? Is there a free trial?

Your first report is free. After that, each report costs 1 credit. You can buy a pack of 2 credits for $0.99 or 15 credits for $4.99, which never expire, or subscribe for $9.99 per month for 50 credits. The monthly plan can be cancelled anytime.

Can Thesis write an investment thesis for a stock?

Yes. Search a US-listed stock and Thesis researches it and writes the investment thesis for you: a clear view, the key points that support or temper it, a bull, base and bear valuation, and the risks that would change the call. Thesis trackers ask you to write the thesis yourself and then monitor it. Thesis builds it from the evidence.

How reliable is Thesis's AI stock analysis?

Every report follows the same rules. Fair value is calculated from bull, base and bear scenarios with every assumption shown. The case for and against the stock is weighed before the verdict, the key risks are rated by stakes and likelihood, and missing data is left out instead of invented. When the evidence is thin, the conviction level drops. No research can predict the market, and AI-generated research can contain errors, so treat every verdict as one input to your own decision.

Where does the data come from?

Reports are built from current company financial statements and filings, the latest earnings call transcripts, market and price data, analyst estimates, public disclosures of congressional trades and fund holdings, and up-to-date news and web research.

How is Thesis different from a stock screener or a general AI chatbot?

A stock screener filters companies by numbers. A general chatbot answers from whatever it remembers. Thesis runs a structured research process on current data for one company at a time, values the business, weighs the strongest case against it, spells out the key risks, and only then reaches a verdict.
